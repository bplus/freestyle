Freestyle Builder

Support

Freestyle Builder is a free, offline practice app for freestyle rap, made by one person.

Contact

Questions or bug reports: freestyle@bplus.dev. I read everything, but this is a hobby project, so replies may be slow.

Common questions

The app says "Mic access off." Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and turn on Freestyle Builder.

Where are my recordings? Only on your phone, inside the app (Past Sessions). They are not backed up to any server by the app.
