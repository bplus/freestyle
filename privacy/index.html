Freestyle Builder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Short version

Freestyle Builder collects no data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to me or anyone else.

Details

  • Microphone: used only to record your practice sessions. Recordings are saved on your device and never uploaded.
  • Your sessions and word packs are stored only on your device.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. The app does not use the internet.
  • Deleting data: delete a session in the app (Past Sessions, swipe to delete) to remove its recording, or delete the app to remove everything.

If your device backs up app data to iCloud, that backup is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings, not by this app.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, a future version adds an online feature), this page will be updated before that version ships.
